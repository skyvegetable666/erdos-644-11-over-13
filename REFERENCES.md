# References

1. P. Erdos, D. G. Fon-Der-Flaass, A. V. Kostochka, and Z. Tuza,
   *Small transversals in uniform hypergraphs*, Siberian Advances in
   Mathematics **2**(1) (1992), 82-88. The original problem source.

2. D. G. Fon-Der-Flaass, A. V. Kostochka, and D. R. Woodall,
   *Transversals in uniform hypergraphs with property (7,2)*,
   Discrete Mathematics **207** (1999), 277-284.
   [DOI: 10.1016/S0012-365X(99)00114-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0012-365X(99)00114-4).
   Page 278 explicitly recalls $f(r,6,2)=r$ for $r\geq2$, which is
   the only previously published upper-bound theorem used in this
   release's small-rank step. The paper also proves an upper bound
   with asymptotic coefficient $7/8$ and a finite improvement of the
   complete-family lower bound.

3. T. F. Bloom, [Erdos Problem #644](https://www.erdosproblems.com/644),
   accessed 2026-10-29. The current problem statement and source index.

The published small-rank fact was checked in the 1999 primary source.
The original 1992 article is cited through that source and the problem
index; a new source-level review of the full 1992 article has not been
performed for this release. The journal PDFs are not redistributed here.

An earlier research search also encountered a public claim of a $6/7$
coefficient whose proof completeness was not established. It is not a
dependency of this release. No claim of priority, novelty, or an exhaustive
current literature survey is made.
